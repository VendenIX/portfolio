I'm Romain, an AI engineer working at the intersection of deep learning and clinical oncology. I'm currently on a fixed-term contract at the **Centre François Baclesse** (Caen) contributing to a national **federated learning** project for glioblastoma survival prediction, building on the explainability work from my previous internship there.

### research focus

My work centers on making deep learning models trustworthy enough for real clinical use. During two research internships at CFB, I helped deploy a UNETR-based model for automatic brain metastases segmentation — [co-first author on the resulting paper]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) in *NeuroImage* — and then built a multi-layered explainability pipeline (Grad-CAM, LRP, LIME, linear probing, causal activation patching) to audit a glioblastoma treatment-response model, uncovering that it relied on a spurious shortcut rather than genuine tumoral features. That [first-author manuscript]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) is currently under review.

I hold a Master's degree in **AI, Data Analysis and Health Access** from the University of Caen, as part of the SATIN project (Health, Territory, Innovation and Digital Technology).

### beyond research

Outside of work, I maintain a self-hosted FreeBSD NAS/cloud server, tinker with Docker/Kubernetes clusters, and keep a running technology watch on new developments in AI and medicine. I'm always happy to talk explainable AI, medical imaging, or potential collaborations — see the contact links below.
